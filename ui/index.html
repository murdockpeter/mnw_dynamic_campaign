Pathing Model

Believable Geometry

The generator does not ask for AI route writing. Each theater carries predefined player, enemy, support, and air or helo corridors. The wizard then seeds deterministic jitter, contact datums, escorts, and withdrawal legs onto those corridors.

Scenario two is not a random reset. It inherits the same route family, advances the clock, tightens density, and shifts spawn and transit points enough to feel like an evolving operation instead of a copy.

Platform Selection

Side Templates

Theater choice drives platform defaults. The player unit, faction, platform type, DBID, starting ammunition, and the opposing force roster all come from the selected theater template.

Campaign climate sets the starting political and operational state for the arc. It biases whether the package begins as restrained patrol work, high-alert interdiction, or open combat.

Mission type tells the generator what operational problem to emphasize. Some options are fully supported now, others are gated mission types. Spec Ops, Counter-Piracy, and Counter-Terror use bespoke experimental objectives; Land Attack remains a clearly labeled future placeholder.

Mission stance is the player-agency bias for the generated package. It tells the generator whether to favor forward intercepts, quiet offset shadowing, broader search geometry, or support-backed barrier play.

Rules of engagement are separate from mission stance. The briefing should tell you both what command wants you to do and what you are actually authorized to shoot.
