Pathing Model

Believable Geometry

The generator does not ask for AI route writing. Each theater carries predefined player, enemy, support, and air or helo corridors. The wizard then seeds deterministic jitter, contact datums, escorts, and withdrawal legs onto those corridors.

Scenario two is not a random reset. It inherits the same route family, advances the clock, tightens density, and shifts spawn and transit points enough to feel like an evolving operation instead of a copy.

Platform Selection

Side Templates

Theater choice drives platform defaults. The player unit, faction, platform type, DBID, starting ammunition, and the opposing force roster all come from the selected theater template.

Tone selection changes mission archetype order, not national sides. That keeps the generated campaign coherent while still varying the sequence of scout, route-bend, containment, and closing scenarios.

Mission posture is the player-agency bias for the generated package. It tells the generator whether to favor forward intercepts, quiet offset shadowing, broader search geometry, or support-backed barrier play.
